Privacy Policy — Shukrona food
------------------------------

Last updated: October 27, 2026

### Introduction
This privacy policy explains what information the Shukrona food mobile app ("the App") collects, how it is used, and what choices you have. The App is provided by Shukrona food free of charge and runs on the Mison restaurant platform. By using the App you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

### Information We Collect
* **Phone number** — used to sign in with a one-time SMS code and to contact you about your order.
* **Name and date of birth** — provided by you at registration; used to address you and, where the restaurant offers them, for birthday promotions. Date of birth may be optional.
* **Delivery addresses** — the addresses you save, used to deliver your orders.
* **Location** — with your permission, the device location is used to show the menu of the nearest branch and to choose a delivery address on the map. Location is used only while the App is open and is not used for tracking.
* **Order history** — items, amounts, payment type and order status, so that you can view and repeat your orders.
* **Device information** — app version, platform and a push notification token, used to deliver order status notifications and to keep the App up to date.

We do not collect contacts, photos, files or payment card data. Online payments, if available, are processed by the payment provider on its own page.

### How We Use Information
Information is used only to provide the service: accepting and delivering orders, showing order status, loyalty points and promotions, customer support and improving the App. We do not sell your personal information.

### Third-Party Services
The App uses third-party services that may process data on our behalf:
* Mison (restaurant management platform, order processing and data storage);
* Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications);
* Yandex MapKit (maps and address search);
* SMS providers (delivery of sign-in codes).

### Data Retention and Deletion
Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the App: Profile → My data → Delete account. After deletion your personal data is removed, except for information we must keep by law (for example, fiscal records of completed orders).

### Security
We use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

### Children's Privacy
The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

### Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page and take effect immediately after posting.
